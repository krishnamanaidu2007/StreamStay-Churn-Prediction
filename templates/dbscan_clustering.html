1. Clustering Parameters

DBSCAN identifies dense groups and flags customers outside those groups as noise. It uses the seven scaled numerical customer-behavior features on a reproducible sample of up to 2,500 customers; IDs and is_churn are excluded.

Leave EPS blank to use the automatic neighbor-distance recommendation, or enter 0.05–10. Minimum Samples must be 2–50. Each submitted setting creates separate cached results and charts.
