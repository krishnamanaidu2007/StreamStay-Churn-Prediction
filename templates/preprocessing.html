5. Categorical Encoding

One-Hot Encoding is applied with handle_unknown="ignore", so unseen categories do not break later transformations.

6. Feature Scaling

Numerical features use StandardScaler. The scaler is fitted only on X_train, then used to transform X_test to prevent leakage.
