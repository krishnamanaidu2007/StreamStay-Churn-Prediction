Uses seven scaled numerical subscription features on a reproducible sample of up to 5,000 customers. Customer IDs and is_churn are excluded; PCA is used only to display the completed groups.

Silhouette selects the most separated tested groups; Elbow selects the strongest bend in WCSS; Manual uses your selected K. Every choice has its own cached result and charts.
